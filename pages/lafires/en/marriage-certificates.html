Learn how to replace lost or destroyed marriage records from California. Replacements are free of charge for verified applicants in disaster-affected zip codes.

If the marriage happened in another state, learn how to get records from other states.

Apply online

To apply online, you’ll need to verify your identity with either a Login.gov account or by signing a sworn statement in front of a notary.

Verify your identity with Login.gov

Login.gov is a secure, federally backed service for identity verification that provides access to many U.S. government websites with one login.

  • Sign in or create an account with Login.gov.
  • Once verified, you can submit your application.

Get started with

Apply by mail

Print the application for a replacement birth record.

  • PDF versions are available from the California Department of Public Health.
  • Fill it out, notarize it, and mail it according to the instructions.

For the PDFs and addresses, visit California Department of Public Health - Vital Records.

Apply by email

Select your application below to get started. After filling it out, print the form, sign it in front of a notary, and upload the signed application to submit online.

  • Birth record. (English)
  • Registros de nacimiento. (Español)

Other options

  • The county recorder’s office in the county where the birth occurred.
