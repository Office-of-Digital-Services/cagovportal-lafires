If you lost IDs or other personal documents in the fires, learn how to get replacements.

Most replacement documents come by mail. If your mailing address has changed, set up mail forwarding.

Licenses and IDs

  • Driver's license or California ID card (no fee for cards lost in the fires)
  • Social Security card
  • U.S. passport
  • Military ID card

Birth, death, and marriage certificates (vital records)

Records from California

  • Birth certificates
  • Marriage certificates
  • Death certificates

Records from other states

  • Vital records (CDC)

Immigration documents

  • Naturalization or citizenship documents
  • Green Card (Permanent Resident Card)

Vehicle documents

  • Vehicle titles (no fees for titles lost in the fires)
  • Mobile home registrations and titles

To replace license plates and other vehicle documents, go to DMV online services.
