Support for businesses

Business disaster loans

The Small Business Association (SBA) offers disaster loans to help businesses. The deadline to apply for loans to repair damage to your business was March 31, 2025.

The deadline to apply for a SBA loan to cover economic losses was October 25, 2025.

Grants and other recovery resources

Find grants, loans, and other resources for businesses and workers impacted by the wildfires. This includes financial help, recovery programs, and workforce support.

Tax help and relief

Business income tax

Businesses impacted by the wildfires may postpone filing and paying income taxes. The deadline was October 15, 2025. Learn about the income tax extension.

Only Los Angeles County was granted postponement. If your principal place of business is outside of Los Angeles County, you must file and pay by the normal due dates.

Sales tax and fees

Get emergency relief from penalties and interest. You can also get copies of tax records and up to a 3-month filing extension on your taxes.

Payroll tax

Employers in Los Angeles and Ventura Counties directly affected by the Palisades Fire may request up to a two-month extension. Learn how to request an extension to report and pay payroll taxes.

Property tax

If your property was affected by the fires, you can:

  • Postpone paying your property tax until April 10, 2026. Learn about the property tax extension.
  • Apply for property tax relief from Los Angeles County.
